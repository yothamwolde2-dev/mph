# Changelog

## 0.9.3, 19 October 2026

* Dahua book: every product is a 3D model again, now drawn to match Dahua's own photos: the sculpted white and black ITC439 and ITC215, the bullet camera with its sun shield and wall bracket, the grey ITC413 with its junction box, the ITC952 with its illuminator, the gunmetal ITC314 drum, the Spotter Nano, the NVR with its real rear panel, and the door stations, face terminal and guidance sign carrying the real product faces. Truer colours and softer light.

## 0.9.2, 19 October 2026

* Dahua book: the first real 3D models, made from Dahua's own product photos, so shape and texture match the product: the ITC439 outdoor parking detector and the ITC952 enforcement camera. Open either and it turns a full 360. More products follow as they are generated and checked.

## 0.9.1, 19 October 2026

* Dahua book: the drawn 3D models are gone. Products show Dahua's own photos again until real 3D models made from those photos are added. The viewer now loads textured model files (GLB) per product from content/models, turns them a full 360 and falls back to the photos.

## 0.9.0, 19 October 2026

* Dahua book, products in 3D: every camera, detector, recorder, terminal, door station, hub, barrier, sign and charger is now a 3D model drawn with three.js and lit like a studio shot. Open a product and it turns a full 360; drag to turn it, scroll to zoom, tap 360 to turn again. Dahua's own photos are still one tap away under Product photos.
* New chapter, Installation, from survey to handover: eight stages with who does what, an ANPR lane designer (height, distance, offset, resolution, with angles and pixels on the plate), a parking guidance planner, a PoE power budget, a commissioning checklist that is saved, and installer notes per product.
* New chapter, City Brain and DataV, explained: the path of one signal from a Dahua device to City Brain and the DataV wall, one event end to end, four animated use cases (adaptive signals, emergency priority, incident detection, citywide parking with pay after leaving), DataV in four steps, a live DataV style wall, and the three real integration paths.
* New chapter, Where it runs today: a 3D globe with Hangzhou, Macau, Kuala Lumpur, the Meskel Square car park in Addis Ababa, a Danish airport and the Polish Tricity, and Hangzhou year by year from 2016 to 2020. Thirteen new sources.
* Polish: no dashes or divider lines anywhere in the book, the roadmap no longer overlaps on iPhone, softer cards, pill tabs and buttons.

## 0.8.3, 19 October 2026

* Reminders on the Plan page: add one with a day, a time and a repeat (once, every day, every weekday, every week, every month), or from any task's menu with Remind me. They show in the Reminders panel, on their day in the week board and in Today on Home.
* Notifications: turn them on per device in Settings, Notifications. Reminders then arrive on the iPhone and the Mac even when MPH is closed (on iPhone, open MPH from its Home Screen icon first; iOS 16.4 or later). While MPH is open, a reminder also rings in the app with a soft chime, Done and Snooze 10 minutes.
* Project reminders now ring too, at your morning time, and a daily briefing each morning tells you how many tasks you have and your top priorities. Both are set in Settings.

## 0.8.0, 18 October 2026

* German A1 and Afaan Oromo Level 1 are now real courses, not only flashcards. A new Course tab teaches in order: 16 German lessons and 10 Afaan Oromo lessons, each with goals, explanations, phrases with sound, tables, culture notes, the lesson's words, its grammar, its dialogs and readings, exercises, a writing task saved to your account, a five question check and recommended YouTube videos.
* Afaan Oromo has sound: a computer voice for every word, example, dialog line and phrase (660 clips), following Qubee spelling. Tap any highlighted word in a lesson to hear it. New grammar: the subject form, adjectives, numbers, joining ideas, this and that, wanting and being able. Three new dialogs and five new exercise sets.
* New Progress tab for every language level: how much of the level is done and what is left, lessons, words mastered, exercises passed, dialogs, your rhythm over four weeks, your hard words (every word you mark Again or Hard until you master it) with a practice deck, and every question you got wrong, to repeat until it is right. Export as a designed report (HTML, print to PDF) and your hard words as a list for Excel or Numbers.
* A Back button at the top of every screen, on Mac and iPhone, that returns to the screen you came from.
* Earlier versions: MPH now keeps a daily copy of your workspace in your database for 30 days (Settings, Data, Earlier versions), and you can bring any of them back.
* Offline copy of MPH: one file that opens by double click and still signs in to your data (Settings, Data). Built by tools/standalone.py.
