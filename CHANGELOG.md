# Changelog

## 0.6.1, 18 October 2026

* A clear Back button in every book, manual, document and language lesson, on Mac and iPhone. From a chapter it returns to the contents, from the contents to where you opened the book (Library, a course page or Home).
* On iPhone the reading bar puts the title on its own line so nothing is squeezed.

## 0.6.0, 18 October 2026

* Library has five courses in five levels: International Business, Project Management, Finance, Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence. Each opens on its own page with the scope (what it covers, what it does not, how it works, which standards it follows) and the journey from Foundation to Practitioner, Professional, Specialist and Expert.
* Levels 1 and 2 are open for all five, 120 chapters in total. Every chapter runs Learn, Understand, See it in practice, Practice with a model answer, Decide, a quiz, take aways and a private reflection. Each level ends with case studies, simulations, worked exercises, templates, a glossary and a mastery checklist. Levels 3 to 5 are shown locked.
* New Exercises section in study books, with self marking.
* All ten books open offline.

## 0.5.0, 18 October 2026

* German A1 is open: 629 words with articles, plurals and verb forms, 34 grammar points covering the whole A1 syllabus, 15 dialogs, 8 reading and listening texts (emails, adverts, announcements), and 32 exercise sets with 203 questions (choose or type the answer, with explanations and audio). A2 and B1 are shown locked.
* Afaan Oromo Level 1 is open: 195 words, 14 grammar points (including Ethiopian time), 6 dialogs and 8 exercise sets. Devices have no Afaan Oromo voice, so it is a reading course; Level 2 is shown locked.
* New Exercises tab for languages; your best score per set is saved and synced.
* The language screens now work for any language: labels, voices and fonts follow the language.

## 0.4.0, 17 October 2026

* New Plan page replaces Tasks and Week. It cascades from the month to the day: the month's focus (up to five outcomes), the Big 3 for the week (each can support a month goal), a to do list for the week, every day of the week with its tasks, reminders and project items due, and a weekly review (what went well, what got in the way, focus for next week) with the week's score.
* Star up to three top priorities a day. Unfinished tasks from earlier days wait in Today with Move to today. Move any task to another day, to this week, or to next week from its menu, or drag it on the Mac. Tap a task's text to edit it.
* Home: today's tasks with quick add, a month calendar (tap a day to plan it), and sticky notes in Mansen colours. Tapping MPH goes Home.
* Projects: priority shows as a numbered badge (1 green, 2 white, 3 grey), click it to change. Due dates are optional. Sub items are light: status, add to plan, notes, reminders, delete. Every item has an Add to tasks button (Today, Tomorrow, This week); finishing the task marks the item Done.
* Menu: Home, Projects, Plan, Learning, Library.
* Delete buttons use a bin icon. Backup export includes reading and course progress.

## 0.3.0, 17 October 2026

* Library is now your bookshelf: Corporate Management, the Coffee Sourcing and Export Intelligence manual and The Intelligent District (Dahua) as covers. Learning keeps languages and courses.
* The Coffee manual and the Dahua proposal keep their own design and every interactive tool (origin explorer, price calculator, checklists, glossary, parking and traffic simulations), with MPH bookmarks, highlights, contents, search and resume on top. Checklists and forms you fill in are saved and synced.
* Bookmarks, highlights and reading position now merge between Mac and iPhone instead of one device overwriting the other. A Mac that stays open picks up what you did on the iPhone when you come back to it.
* Your place is saved the moment you leave the app.
* Mac keyboard in books: B bookmarks, H highlights the selection, arrows move between sections.
* MPH updates itself: a new version is picked up when you open the app and switched in at the next page change.
* Home shows your books with Continue reading.

## 0.2.0, 17 October 2026

* New Learning section, built natively for speed: no uploads needed, everything opens instantly and works offline.
* Languages: Chinese with HSK 2 (from the Tingdong lab) and a new HSK 3 course with 307 words, 16 long dialogs, 4 listening passages and 24 grammar points. HSK 4 and HSK 5 are locked. German and Afaan Oromo are ready as places for future courses.
* Flashcards with spaced repetition in four decks: read, listen, recall and grammar patterns, with audio from the device's Chinese voice.
* Dialog player with play all, speed, listen only mode, pinyin and English toggles, and comprehension questions.
* Listening drills: hear the word, hear the sentence, dialog challenge, listening passages. Missed words return to the Listen deck.
* Study book reader for Corporate Management Book 1: contents, visible bookmark ribbons, resume to the exact paragraph, highlights with notes, search, glossary, cases, skills, frameworks, templates, simulations and quizzes.
* Private content: course and book files are locked with a key that only your login can read.

## 0.1.3, 16 October 2026

* Reader: the document area is pinned to the full space, fixing the black screen in Safari.
* Reader: reading progress follows one scroll area, so the percentage no longer jumps.

## 0.1.2, 16 October 2026

* Much faster: the app opens from the copy kept on the device and updates itself in the background.
* Documents download once and then open instantly from the device.
* Screen redraws are combined, the page fade plays only when you change page, and saving status no longer redraws the screen.
* Saving to the device happens in the background so taps never wait.

## 0.1.1, 16 October 2026

* New reader tools: resume where you left off, bookmarks with notes, highlights with notes, contents, search inside the document, text size, reading progress on every tile, focus mode.
